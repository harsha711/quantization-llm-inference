# Optimization decision map

This is a decision aid, not an implementation queue. Candidate mechanisms are hypotheses until isolated by measurements. Read [EXPERIMENTS.md](EXPERIMENTS.md) before proposing work. Implement only the experiment requested by the user.

E001 is complete, and E002 specialization is accepted against its predeclared E001 comparison. E002 removes the measured guard overhead without changing the mapping/layout or tail correctness. M=32/128 gains exceed 1.05 under both policies, while M=1 does not regress. E002 remains slightly slower than naive at M=128, so it is not a universal replacement. No further optimization is authorized. Weight reuse across M rows is a candidate to inspect next if requested, but persistent L2 traffic does not alone establish the critical bottleneck.

Do not promote every row to a bottleneck explanation. If a trial fails, record why and its revisit condition in the experiment log. A changed compiler, GPU, cache policy, numerical contract, or data layout may justify a new trial only after its effect on the baseline is measured consistently.
